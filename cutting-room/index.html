Editor's rules of thumb

  • Hit the beats. Each slot on the strip is a beat the director asked for.
  • Don't cross the line. Arrows show which way someone moves or looks. Keep the 180° geography consistent.
  • No jump cuts. Don't cut between two shots of the same subject at the same size.
  • Mind the runtime. Land within two seconds of the director's target.
  • Use Kuleshov. Meaning comes from what a face is cut against. Some pairings earn a bonus. Some backfire.
